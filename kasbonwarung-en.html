Privacy Policy — BukuKasbon

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Contact: Fikinur@ciplux.com

BukuKasbon is a warung credit-ledger app developed by PT CIPLUX MAHDANI SOLUTION. You record customers, credit entries (kasbon), and payments, and the app computes each customer's outstanding balance. The app can be used without creating an account and works fully offline.

1. Data We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal information on our servers — this app has no server and no internet permission, so no data can leave your device from the app. The app also contains no analytics SDK or advertising SDK in the current version.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored only on your device (the app's local database and storage):

  • Customers you record: name, phone number (optional, for WhatsApp reminders), and notes.
  • Credit entries: amount, notes, and due date if any.
  • Payment entries: amount, notes, and payment date.
  • Labels, archive status, and app settings.

This data never leaves your device except through sharing or export actions you perform yourself, as described in section 4.

3. Permissions Used

The app requests no Android permissions in the current version. It does not use notifications; reminders to customers are sent via WhatsApp only when you tap them yourself (see section 4).

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with any third party and we do not sell user data. The app connects to no third-party service, except for actions you start yourself as follows:

You can export a data backup in JSON format from Settings. The backup file is stored in the app's folder on your device and is fully under your control; the app does not upload it anywhere. Restoring a backup merges with existing data — old data is not overwritten.

If you tap a WhatsApp reminder or share action, the app opens a WhatsApp link (wa.me) with the number and message you see; from that point, that data is subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy. The app does not send messages on its own without your tap.

A one-time Pro purchase, when available, is processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your card details. Pro status is recorded locally on your device.

If in the future we add third-party services, we will update this privacy policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosure before releasing such an update.

5. Children

This app is a general-purpose utility app and is not intended to collect children's data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Storage and Deletion of Data

Because we store no personal data on a server, there is no personal data of yours to delete from our server. Data stored on your device can be deleted by clearing the app's data in device settings or by uninstalling the app. Removing a customer inside the app archives it rather than permanently deleting it from the database, unless you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will post the latest version on this page and update the effective date above.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:
