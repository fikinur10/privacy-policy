Privacy Policy — Kalkulator Zakat & THR

Effective date: 20 October 2026 · Contact: fiknur@ciplux.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Kalkulator Zakat & THR ("the App") handles information. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Developer and contact

Developer: Ciplux Apps (Ciplux Mahdani Solution)
Country: Indonesia
Contact email: fiknur@ciplux.com

For questions about this Privacy Policy, or to request deletion of data associated with your use of the App, contact us at the email above.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores your calculation history locally on your device. This may include:

  • Calculation inputs, such as savings, gold value, debts, number of family members, salary, and length of service.
  • Calculation results and timestamps.
  • App settings, such as selected language and gold reference price.

This information is stored only on your device. The App does not upload, transmit, or store this calculation data on our servers.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The App displays advertisements using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect certain information to provide, personalize, and measure ads, such as device information, approximate location, advertising identifiers, and interactions with advertisements.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You may manage ad personalization through your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads personalization.

4. Permissions

The App does not require access to sensitive personal data such as contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or files.

5. Data security

Calculation history is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal calculation data. Third-party advertising services use their own security measures, as described in their privacy policies.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your calculation history remains on your device until you delete it through the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored App data.

Data collected by Google AdMob is subject to Google's retention practices and your device or Google account settings.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
