Privacy Policy — NovelEngine

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Contact: fiknur@ciplux.com

NovelEngine is a novel and e-book reader app, developed by PT CIPLUX MAHDANI SOLUTION. You can read titles from the app's built-in catalog and import your own book files (EPUB and TXT) to read inside the app. The app works fully offline — DRM-protected books cannot be opened and are rejected with a clear message. The app works without an account.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, the contents of books you import, or any other personal information to our servers — this app has no server and holds no internet permission, so no data can be sent out of your device. The app also contains no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

The only third-party service involved is Google Play, for the in-app unlock purchase of certain catalog titles: the payment transaction is processed entirely by Google Play and we do not receive your payment card details. Books you import yourself are never gated by any purchase.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored only on your device (in the app's local database and storage):

  • Book files you import and their basic metadata (title, author, format).
  • Reading progress, bookmarks, and your library list.
  • App settings and the catalog unlock status.

This data never leaves your device.

3. Permissions the App Uses

This app uses no Android permissions at all. You import books through the system file picker, so the app receives only the individual files you choose — the app has no general access to your storage.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with any third party and we do not sell user data. Apart from purchase processing by Google Play as described in section 1, this app is not connected to any third-party service.

If we ever add a third-party service in the future, we will update this privacy policy and the Data Safety disclosure on Google Play before releasing that update.

5. Children

This app is a reader app for general users and is not directed at collecting data from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we store no personal data on a server, there is no personal data of yours for us to delete from a server. Data stored on your device — including imported books and reading progress — can be removed from inside the app, by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will post the latest version on this page and update the effective date above.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:
