Privacy Policy — StoreShot

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Contact: fiknur@ciplux.com

StoreShot is a tool for creating screenshot slides for app store listing pages (Google Play and the App Store), developed by PT CIPLUX MAHDANI SOLUTION. You import screenshots, add headline text, choose a background and device frame, then export the slides as PNG files. The app works without an account and is fully functional offline.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, or any other personal information to our servers — this app has no server and holds no internet permission, so no data can be sent out of your device. The app also contains no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in its current version.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored only on your device (in the app's local database and storage):

  • The projects and slides you create, including headline texts and language variants for each project.
  • Screenshots you import through the system photo picker — copies are stored in the app's storage.
  • App settings and Pro status.

This data never leaves your device except through a share or export action that you perform yourself, as described in section 4.

3. Permissions the App Uses

This app uses the following Android permissions, only for the purposes described:

  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 / API 28 and below only) — used only on older Android versions, at export time, to save exported PNG slides to your device gallery through MediaStore (the Pictures/StoreShot folder). On Android 10 and above this permission is not needed. Screenshot import uses the system photo picker, so the app does not request permission to read your media.

You grant permissions through the system dialog and can revoke them at any time in your device settings; features that depend on a revoked permission will stop working.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with any third party and we do not sell user data. This app is not connected to any third-party service.

Exported slides are saved as PNG files in your device gallery. Anything you do with those files afterwards — including uploading them to a store console — you do yourself outside the app.

If we ever add a third-party service in the future, we will update this privacy policy and the Data Safety disclosure on Google Play before releasing that update.

5. Children

This app is a general utility app and is not directed at collecting data from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we store no personal data on a server, there is no personal data of yours for us to delete from a server. Data stored on your device can be removed by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will post the latest version on this page and update the effective date above.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:
