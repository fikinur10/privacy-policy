Privacy Policy — BirthdayFrames

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Contact: fiknur@ciplux.com

BirthdayFrames is a birthday photo frame app, developed by PT CIPLUX MAHDANI SOLUTION. You pick a photo through the system photo picker, place it into a birthday frame, then export it as a square (1:1) full-resolution PNG image up to 4096 pixels. The app works fully offline on your device and works without an account.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information to our servers — this app has no server. The app also contains no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

The only third-party service involved is Google Play, for the in-app purchase of the premium frame pack: the payment transaction is processed entirely by Google Play and we do not receive your payment card details. Google Play may provide the purchase confirmation status to the app so the frame pack you bought is unlocked.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Photos you pick are processed only on your device. The following data is created and stored only on your device:

  • App settings and the ownership status of the premium frame pack.
  • Exported images that you save yourself to your gallery or a folder of your choice through the system file picker.

Exported files are rewritten from the pixels of the edited image, so metadata embedded in the original photo such as EXIF and GPS location is not carried into the exported file. Your photos are never uploaded anywhere.

3. Permissions the App Uses

This app uses no Android permissions at all. You pick photos through the system photo picker (Android Photo Picker), so the app receives only the individual photos you choose — the app has no general access to your gallery or storage, and it does not request a storage permission.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with any third party and we do not sell user data. Apart from purchase processing by Google Play as described in section 1, this app is not connected to any third-party service.

You can share exported images through the system share sheet — only through your own actions.

If we ever add a third-party service in the future, we will update this privacy policy and the Data Safety disclosure on Google Play before releasing that update.

5. Children

This app is a general utility app and is not directed at collecting data from children. Photos you pick stay on your device; we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we store no personal data on a server, there is no personal data of yours for us to delete from a server. Data stored on your device can be removed by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Images you have exported can be deleted by you from your gallery.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will post the latest version on this page and update the effective date above.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:
