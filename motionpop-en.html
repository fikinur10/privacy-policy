Privacy Policy — MotionPop

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Contact: fiknur@ciplux.com

MotionPop is an animated text and video maker for iOS developed by PT CIPLUX MAHDANI SOLUTION. You pick a template, edit text, preview the animation live, then export short MP4 clips (vertical 1080×1920 in the free version; horizontal 1920×1080 and square 1080×1080 formats with the Pro unlock) with automatic quality checks. Pro users can also export a companion CSV metadata file for stock upload. The app works without an account and is fully functional offline.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, or any other personal information to our servers — this app has no server, contains no analytics SDK, contains no advertising SDK, and contains no third-party SDK of any kind; it uses only Apple's built-in system frameworks. The only network activity that may occur is the one-time Pro purchase and purchase restore, which are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit); we do not see, collect, or store your payment details.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored only on your device:

  • Your projects — the project name, the text you type (title and subtitle), template choice, colors, effects, text size, duration, export format, and the stock metadata you enter (title, keywords, category, and releases note) — stored as a JSON file in the app's Documents folder.
  • Exported files — the MP4 video and, for Pro users, its companion CSV metadata file — stored in the app's Exports folder.
  • Pro unlock status (if you purchase it), cached locally so it keeps working offline.

This data never leaves your device except through a share or export action that you perform yourself, as described in section 4.

3. Permissions the App Uses

This app requests no special permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, photo library, location, or contacts, and it does not read photos or other files from your device — it works entirely with the text you type and the templates built into the app. Exported videos and CSV files are shared only through the iOS system share sheet when you choose to share them.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with any third party and we do not sell user data. This app is not connected to any third-party service other than Apple's App Store for the Pro purchase.

Exported videos and CSV files contain text and metadata you entered yourself — content you share or upload (for example to Adobe Stock) is your responsibility, as is customary for editor apps.

If we ever add a third-party service in the future, we will update this privacy policy and the App Store privacy details before releasing that update.

5. Children

This app is a general creative utility and is not directed at collecting data from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we store no personal data on a server, there is no personal data of yours for us to delete from a server. Projects can be deleted inside the app (project list); all app data can be removed by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will post the latest version on this page and update the effective date above.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:
